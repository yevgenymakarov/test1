The Cavalry script with various Shape tools.

<img width="600" alt="Shape Tools" src="../images/shape-tools.png">

## User Manual

**Swap Shape's Fill/Stroke** - Swap the Fill and Stroke color values, connections and animations of the selected Shape.

**Disable Shape's Fill** - Disable the Fill for the selected Shapes. Hold <kbd>Option/Alt</kbd> to enable the Shape's Fill.

**Disable Shape's Stroke** - Disable the Stroke for the selected Shapes. Hold <kbd>Option/Alt</kbd> to enable the Shape's Stroke.

**Set Stroke Cap and Join Styles to Round** - For the selected Layers, set the Stroke's Cap Style and Join Style to Round.

**Swap Colors Between Shapes** - Swap the Fill and Stroke colors of the two selected Shapes. If more than two Shapes are selected, the colors are shifted in sequence. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to swap only the Stroke colors, or hold <kbd>Option/Alt</kbd> to swap only the Fill colors.
